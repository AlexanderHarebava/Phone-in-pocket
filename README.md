## Phone in Pocket
is a minimalist Android app that creates a tactile connection with your device through regular vibration signals. Designed to work in the background, it allows you to "feel" your phone in your pocket without having to fumble through your pockets.

## Key Features

*   **Customizable Interval:** Choose vibration frequency from 1 to 600 seconds (presets: 3s, 5s, 10s, 15s, 30s, 60s).
*   **6 Unique Patterns:**
    *   *Click:* A short system click.
    *   *Tick:* A subtle tick, like a wristwatch.
    *   *Double:* Two quick pulses.
    *   *Heavy Click:* A firm, distinctly palpable signal.
    *   *Pulse:* A long, smooth vibration.
    *   *Heartbeat:* A rhythmic "thump-thump."
*   **Background Operation:** Reliable service ensuring the timer keeps running even when the screen is off.
*   **Notifications:** Activity status and a stop button right in the notification shade.
*   **Bilingual Support:** English and Russian languages supported.
*   **Modern UI:** Jetpack Compose interface featuring a dark theme, animations, and an adaptive clock face.
